Product Card Added State

Four layout directions for confirming add-to-cart without visual clutter

Today, adding a product replaces the button with the full quantity selector. These layouts keep the confidence of “added” while making quantity changes optional and calmer for older or less technical shoppers.

Strongest default

Compact confirmation is the safest first pass: it preserves product-card height and gives explicit Change and Remove actions.

Fastest quantity edits

The inline stepper is useful when quantity changes are common, but it assumes there is a sensible next and previous quantity.

Best for complex quantities

The bottom sheet keeps all existing quantity options, including discounts and returnables, without crowding every product card.
