## REMOVED Requirements

### Requirement: Cart Item Quantity Controls
Product cards displayed units with a dropdown selector for changing unit selection, along with +/- buttons to increment/decrement quantity when an item is already in the cart.

**Reason**: Simplify product card UI to reduce complexity and focus on product display and initial selection.

**Migration**: Quantity and unit management should be handled through a dedicated cart management interface (e.g., cart screen or separate cart controls).

### Requirement: Cart Item Delete Button
Product cards showed a delete/dumpster button to remove items from the cart inline from the product card.

**Reason**: Simplify product card UI and encourage explicit cart management.

**Migration**: Item removal should be handled in a dedicated cart management interface.
