## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Product Card Display
Product cards SHALL display a product image, name, price, and action button for adding to cart. The Add button SHALL be positioned as an overlay on the bottom-right corner of the product image, rather than below the product information section. The button SHALL remain fully functional and visible.

#### Scenario: Product card with overlay button
- **WHEN** a product card is displayed
- **THEN** the product image is visible with the Add button overlaid in the bottom-right corner
- **AND** clicking the button shows the unit selection modal
